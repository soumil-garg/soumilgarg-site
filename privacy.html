This page explains what soumilgarg.com collects when you visit, why, and what you can do about it. I'm Soumil Garg, based in India.

What I collect

No tracking unless you say yes. There is no sign-up form or account to create. Your cookie choice is saved in your browser. Booking, email and hosting are covered below.

When you first arrive, a banner asks whether you accept cookies. If you click Reject or ignore it, no tracking tools load.

If you click Accept, three tools load:

  • Microsoft Clarity records how people scroll and click, with typed text hidden, so I can fix parts of the page that confuse people. It also records which buttons you use, the link or campaign that brought you here, and any errors on the page.

Clarity uses cookies and similar technology. It collects things like your device, browser, rough location from your IP address, and the pages you view. It does not give me your name or email. If you click through to book, Calendly is told which link or campaign you came from.

Booking a call

Calls are booked through Calendly. When you book, Calendly collects the details you type in, such as your name and email, and shares them with me so I can run the call. Calendly's own privacy policy covers how it handles them.

Emails

If you email me, I keep your email and my reply so I can answer you and follow up. I don't sell it or add you to a mailing list.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address when the page loads, under its own privacy policy.

Who else sees the data

Only the companies named on this page: Google, Meta, Microsoft, Calendly and GitHub. Each one handles data under its own policy:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Microsoft: privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement
  • Calendly: calendly.com/privacy
  • GitHub: docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Changing your mind

You can turn the cookies off at any time with the link in the footer, or by clearing this site's cookies and site data in your browser. Turning them off stops new data from being collected.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email soumilgarg1111@gmail.com and I'll reply within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
