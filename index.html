Re: Fernwick and your Meta ads

Hi Maya,

Thanks for the yes. I went through the 11 ads Fernwick is running right now in Meta's Ad Library. Here are the three changes I'd make first.

  1. 1

    Your best ads are getting old

    Your three longest running ads started in June. When the same people see an ad for months, it usually gets more expensive to reach them. Make two fresh versions of your oldest ad: same offer, new opening shot.

  2. 2

    Every ad opens the same way

    9 of your 11 ads open on the bag sitting on a kitchen counter. Someone who scrolled past that once will scroll past it again. Try three new openings, each with a different reason to buy: the crunch up close, the 7 a.m. breakfast it saves, and the short ingredient list read out loud.

  3. 3

    Nobody is eating it on camera

    None of your ads show a real person tasting the granola. For snacks, a short phone video of a customer trying it often beats a polished product shot. Ask three happy customers to film one. I can send you a simple script.

That's it. Use these yourself, they're yours either way. If you'd like me to run them, reply and we'll set up a short call.

Soumil
