What does it cost?

$531 a month. Month to month, paid in advance.

How long until it works?

Give it 45 days for a fair read. Brands with more daily orders get clear answers sooner.

Who runs my account?

I do. You get a WhatsApp group with me and a reply within 12 hours, whatever your time zone.

Whose ad account is it?

Yours. It stays in your Business Manager and I join as a partner.

How many new ads do I get?

At least 30 a month. A new campaign starts with 20 to 30, then about 10 new ones join every week.

Do you work with brands outside India?

Yes. Many of my clients are abroad. Calls and updates run in your time zone.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to your Meta ad account and Shopify, plus every photo and video you have. I take it from there.

What happens on the call?

I walk you through your store's top 3 fixes and 3 ad angles. If we're a fit, we plan the start. If not, you keep the fixes.
